# Code of Conduct

ScrolIQ is an evidence project. Good-faith disagreement, negative results,
failed experiments, and corrections are welcome when they are reproducible and
professionally presented.

Participants must not harass, threaten, demean, discriminate against, dox, or
deliberately expose private information about other participants. Do not use
issues, pull requests, or discussions to pressure people into stronger
scientific claims than the evidence supports.

When contributing or reviewing:

- criticize code, data, claims, and methods rather than people;
- preserve attribution and upstream/community credit;
- disclose material provenance or licensing concerns;
- keep failed experiments and falsification controls visible;
- do not knowingly publish embargoed Challenge submission material;
- do not present AI-generated text or predictions as human-verified evidence.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these norms
and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

For conduct concerns that should not be handled publicly, contact the
maintainer privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.

# Ensemble-independence gate — synthetic controls (2026-10-06)

**Synthetic only. No real checkpoint, scroll or ensemble is measured here.**
The panels are generated by `_synthetic_ancestry()` and `_synthetic_panel()` in
`src/scrollq/ensemble_independence.py`; the numbers below are properties of the
control construction, not detection limits on real papyrus.

- Base commit: `791e454ce7d258403305ec40c6704901273c3f29` (this change adds the
  module on top of it)
- Constants frozen in the module before any output was read: control seed
  `20261006`, 300 bootstrap replicates, 8 permutation trials per ensemble,
  maximum null false-detection rate 0.2. One panel construction changed after
  a first look: the CV-like ensemble's member offsets are now zero-centred,
  because a nonzero mean shifted every unit's mean logit and made predictive
  entropy rank failures with AUROC exactly 0 or 1 inside single-truth strata.
  That was a defect of the synthetic panel, not of the metrics. The control's
  null criterion also changed from one permutation draw to a false-detection
  rate over 16 trials (and its seed from the spec's to a constant) after a
  single draw false-alarmed on 3 of 30 seeds at alpha 0.05, which would have
  invited seed shopping.
- `self-test.json` SHA-256:
  `a70c95c76e68658ae3a053dd0732fca76191833ce38c34164cb38c9d9ba6a693`

## Reproduce

```bash
scroliq-ensemble-independence self-test > self-test.json
```

About 4 seconds on one CPU. `tests/test_ensemble_independence.py` re-runs it
and compares the committed file (booleans exactly, AUROCs within 0.02).

## What `self-test.json` shows

| check | result |
|---|---|
| 5-fold CV partition detected from the inventories alone | true |
| mean pairwise overlap coefficient of the CV partition | 0.75 (each member's 80 units, 60 shared) |
| certified independent witnesses, CV partition | 1 of 5 |
| CV partition accepted as a deep ensemble | no (`dependent`) |
| same-data seed ensemble accepted as a deep ensemble | yes (`independent`) |
| certified independent witnesses, same-data seed ensemble | 1 of 5 (all supervision shared) |
| certified independent witnesses, 5 disjoint subsets | 5 of 5 |

Failure-ranking control (240 units, 60 physical blocks): an ensemble whose
members disagree where they err reaches mutual-information AUROC **0.981**
against failure; an ensemble whose members err in agreement (a CV-like panel
with shared common-mode errors and member-specific offsets) reaches **0.446**,
and its interval does not exclude chance. Permuting units gave 0 false
detections in 16 trials. These are the numbers `evaluate` requires before it
will issue any verdict.

## What this does not show

That CV ensembles rank failures worse than independent ones on real data. The
construction plants that outcome; the point is only that the gate can detect it
when present, does not manufacture it when absent, and counts witnesses from
ancestry. Real-data evidence needs the experiment in
[`docs/ensemble-independence.md`](../../docs/ensemble-independence.md).

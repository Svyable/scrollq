ScrolIQ

Diagnostics for reading Herculaneum scrolls. Find the bottleneck.

ScrolIQ does not try to replace the community's geometry, winding, scan-quality or ink tools. It makes their evidence composable: results are bound to the exact CT, mesh, model and evaluation region they measure; stale or cross-volume evidence is excluded; leakage and missing evidence fail closed; and failed controls stay visible. What is actually differentThe contribution is evidence composition, not ownership of every underlying diagnostic.

Public Vesuvius work already covers substantial parts of scan QA, TIFXYZ integrity/repair, self-intersections, spiral evaluation and held-out ink validation. ScrolIQ's system-level contribution is to bind those measurements to the exact bytes and exact eligible volume they describe, preserve negative results, and prevent missing, stale, cross-volume or train-overlapping evidence from being promoted into a stronger claim.

Identity before confidence

CT root, surface hashes, external reports, checkpoint identity, seeds and evaluation arrays travel with the result. Same scroll name is not enough.

Falsification stays visible

Disjoint resampling, injection controls and held-out checks are allowed to fail. A failed gate narrows the claim instead of disappearing.

One proof before more breadth

The next milestone is one exact Grand Prize volume with a measurable held-out surface / fit / ink improvement and a single passport connecting the evidence. Protocol →

LIVE

Winding annotations

LIVE

On the published PHercParis4 annotations: 2 of 13,700 comparable pairs out of radial order, both under 0.1 voxel; an injection control catches 89.5% of ±3 and 85.5% of ±5 single-point mis-numberings, and ±2 errors are undetectable by design. Evidence contractsWhat a passing diagnostic proves — and what it deliberately leaves unknown.

Mesh IQ

Proves: native TIFXYZ structure/provenance and selected grid-local geometry checks; validated upstream reports can additionally bind CT support and transverse self-intersection evidence. Does not prove: correct sheet identity.

Fiber IQ

Proves: exact-file/native-format provenance plus rendered-line continuity and control-point/render consistency checks when supplied; a pinned 8-fiber PHercParis4 campaign demonstrates the checks on public data. Does not prove: physical fiber identity, one-sheet continuity, exact CT-volume binding for that public campaign, or local CT orientation support.

Winding IQ

Proves: volume-bound PointCollections input integrity, descriptive axial coverage, and (with an umbilicus) radial-order review candidates with a measured recall. Does not prove: patch attachment, consistent cycles, ±1/±2 winding errors, or spiral-fit accuracy.

Ink IQ

Proves: declared spatial separation, checkpoint/seed provenance, and falsification-control coverage. Does not prove: that a prediction is ink.

Passport

Cross-volume artifacts are excluded and failed audits block their stage. A passing component remains partial until the next direct evidence layer exists.

Independent same-byte cross-check

Mesh IQ was run on the exact 1,818,055 SHA-256-verified bytes in TIFXYZ Doctor's pinned 10-case real-data benchmark: 10 / 10 agreement on enclosed-hole presence, 10 / 10 on single-component presence, and 7 / 7 hole-presence agreement on the PHerc0800 + PHerc1447 Grand Prize overlap. The first run also exposed our own isometry normalization error; after fixing it, all 10 / 10 Doctor-below-threshold cases have no Mesh IQ isometry finding, with nearly identical p95 stretch on the Villa controls. Rival benchmark roles are provenance labels, not geometry ground truth. Frozen same-byte evidence →

Cross-tool evidence, content-bound

On PHerc0139 segment 20260306000001-w051_2026030600, ScrolIQ and Windcheck independently name the same published coordinate bytes: full x/y/z SHA-256s, no mask, 379×480 grid, and 164,096 valid vertices agree. Mesh IQ reports 15 edge jumps and 173 normal reversals; Windcheck's same-original nonlocal census reports 3,333 transverse contacts. Binding stops at coordinate-exact, not semantic-exact, because the external release-index projection does not expose the original meta.json hash. Frozen binding-aware dossier →

VC3D jump-to-problem queue

Mesh IQ now emits native VC3D PointCollections instead of leaving reviewers with counts. On the content-bound PHerc0139 surface, the frozen queue contains 35 directly loadable XYZ sites: all 15 / 15 edge jumps plus the top 20 / 173 normal reversals. The same surface is independently bound to Windcheck's 3,333-contact nonlocal census, so local review coordinates and independent nonlocal evidence can be inspected without conflating them. VC3D PointCollections → · Frozen campaign →

Winding check with measured sensitivity

On public PHercParis4 annotations, the ray-order audit found 2 inversions / 13,700 comparable pairs. In a seeded injection control, deliberate ±3 winding errors were detected in 179 / 200 testable trials and ±5 errors in 171 / 200; ±2 is undetectable by construction. That measured blind spot is part of the result. Audit + injection control →

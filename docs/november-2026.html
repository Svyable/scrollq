Where October leaves us

As of 2026-10-04: O2 (winding attachment) and O5 (stability gate → rank bands) are done. O4 part 1 (full fiber census) is done. O6 has guides and three unfiled upstream drafts ready. O1 is waiting on a human VC3D review, and O3 is waiting on a GPU fit. Both carry over below unless they close in October. Goals

N1Close O1: publish Mesh IQ flag precision

real datafailure cases of existing methodscarryover · sample frozenneeds human reviewers

Why. 77 of 307 segments are flagged, but no one has yet measured how many flags are real.

Starting point. Blinded 54-segment sample and key-free packet (mesh-review-sample), protocol frozen in mesh-review-protocol.md.

Deliverable. Returned reviewer labels, scored by bin/mesh_review_score.py as written in the protocol.

Done when. Flag precision and the clean-segment defect rate are committed with 95% intervals. If no review comes back by Nov 21, publish that as the status and keep the rate unknown.

N2Two surface arms on one exact volume, scored blind on the same frozen truth

Grand Prize bottleneckheld-out validationraised 2026-10-04

Why. This is the only goal that moves the bottleneck sentence. The frozen PHerc0139 Phase-A sheetness arm returned FAIL (10 / 32 groups), so one more run of the same idea is not enough. Two independent arms against one truth commitment tell us which direction works.

Starting point. Phase-A FAIL (sheetness-run), the blind TIFXYZ scorer scroliq-segmentation-validate, the target-freeze gate, and the CT fiber-fingerprint development protocol.

Deliverable. One exact prize-eligible volume. One salted truth commitment frozen before any prediction exists. At least two pre-registered surface arms, for example a learned surface predictor and a physical descriptor arm, each scored blind per region with bidirectional coverage and topology gates. Stretch: recto-coverage accounting (scroliq-recto-coverage) over one contiguous multi-column region of the winning arm.

Done when. Both arms have committed verdicts against the same truth, pass or fail, with failed regions listed. A failure does not license retuning under the same identity.

N3Blind evaluations of three community models on one held-out split

gets usedheld-out validationraised 2026-10-04needs model owners

Why. One evaluation is a demo. Three evaluations on the same split are a comparison people can act on.

Starting point. scroliq-eval contracts, overlap blocking, bootstrap CIs and the two-trust-domain design (model-evaluation.md). Zero published evaluations.

Deliverable. At least three public models covering surface and ink. They are evaluated on one declared held-out split with model cards, checkpoint hashes and overlap checks, and published as a comparison table with CIs and failed regions.

Done when. At least two evaluations are committed, with the third either committed or explicitly declined by its owner. Ask each model owner before publishing.

N4Fiber IQ: a span-aware gap rule, a second scroll, and CT direction

real dataopen problem: fiber connectivityraised 2026-10-04

Why. October showed that the whole-fiber gap rule mostly measures fallback rendering density (O7), and the CT support question moved into October (O9).

Deliverable. (a) A span-aware gap rule, evaluated pre-registered against the current rule on the O7 data with a planted-break positive control. (b) A census of the public PHerc0139 fibers, with the same fail-closed listing and controls. (c) CT fiber direction agreement in the O9 volume (structure-tensor orientation vs fiber tangent), with a rotated-fiber control.

Done when. All three have committed verdicts. If the O9 support test did not pass, (c) stays unknown with that reason.

N5Close O3: score a spiral fit on constraints it never saw

open problem: spiral fittingcarryover · evaluator readyneeds a GPU fit run

Why. A fit's own satisfaction score is not a held-out score.

Starting point. The held-out geometry evaluator (heldout-geometry-evaluation.md) and the consumer-GPU gate (spiral-consumer-gpu-gate.md).

Deliverable / done when. One real fit run with declared withheld collections, its config and checkpoint hashes, and the held-out score committed.

N6Exact-volume surface-support survey of all 13 Grand Prize volumes

frozen datainsightful, actionablereplaces the old N6 (done in October as O8)

Why. The v2 frontier (O8) leaves the segment-free slot as a band within noise. A third, independent axis is the honest way to break the tie, and a blended score is not.

Starting point. scroliq-support (exact-scan prediction CT support with bootstrap CIs) and the First Letters support campaign; the October frontier has only two axes.

Deliverable. A new dated campaign that measures surface-prediction CT support on each exact eligible volume where a released prediction exists, and reports the three-axis frontier as a sensitivity analysis. A volume without a released prediction stays unknown.

Done when. Every one of the 13 targets is measured or listed with its reason, and README and docs are updated together.

N7Upstream: from drafts to filed reports, and the dl regression

fixes bugs in tools people use3 drafts readyneeds maintainer approval

Deliverable. After the maintainer reproduces each defect and approves it, file the report. Run the dl regression.

Ground rules (unchanged)

  • Every number traces to a command and a committed artifact. README, docs and artifact READMEs change together.
  • Every checker has a positive control and reports unverified when it inspected nothing.
  • Pre-register, then log every deviation. Failed gates are published.
  • No readability claims from the scan-health score. Exact-volume, fail-closed Grand Prize matching.
  • Dated data is frozen data. Upstream filing and releases need explicit maintainer approval.

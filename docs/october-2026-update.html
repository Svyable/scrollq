Competitive thesis

ScrolIQ does not replace the community's geometry, winding, fiber, scan-quality or ink tools. It makes their evidence composable and submission-grade: each result is bound to the exact CT / mesh / model / evaluation region it measures, leakage and missing evidence fail closed, and independent diagnostics can be carried into one reproducible Grand Prize evidence trail without silently becoming stronger claims than they support.

The October work below matters insofar as it strengthens that contract. The next leap is not another capability category; it is one exact Grand Prize volume with a demonstrably better held-out surface / fit / ink result. The protocol for that focused campaign is here →.

1 Mesh IQ: from 21 meshes to the whole public corpus

  • Grand Prize meshes. All 21 published TIFXYZ meshes for the two targets with public segments, audited against each target's exact eligible CT volume. All 6 PHerc0800 meshes pass; 14 of 15 PHerc1447 meshes are coherent with enclosed holes flagged for review; 20251105093211-z_dbg_gen_00320 is broken (153 disconnected pieces, 1,335 edge jumps, 386 severe normal reversals, p95 local stretch 5.07).
  • Every published segment mesh. 307 segments on 11 scrolls. Tiers count distinct finding kinds: TIFXYZ 189 clean, 462 review, 192 multi-defect; OBJ 47 / 88 / 65. 77 segments are multi-defect on every registration (PHercParis4 27, PHerc0500P2 25, PHerc0814 12, PHerc0172 11, PHerc1447 1, PHerc1667 1). The 60 largest TIFXYZ meshes ran out of memory on a 16 GB runner and are listed with that reason, so the artifact is marked partial.
  • Controls. The hand-confirmed broken PHerc1447 segment is flagged in both formats; all 26 z_dbg-named segments are flagged; TIFXYZ and OBJ agree on 175 of 185 segments.
  • Triangle meshes. scroliq-obj applies the same finding kinds to Wavefront OBJ and adds non-manifold edges, inconsistent winding (kept separate from real folds), degenerate faces and folded-over UV triangles. An OBJ with no faces fails rather than passing.
  • Exact-volume Grand Prize cross-cut. Only 2 of the 13 eligible volumes have published exact-volume segment meshes: PHerc0800 (6 meshes, all clean) and PHerc1447 (15 meshes, 14 review, 1 multi-defect). The other 11 are an evidence gap, not a pass.
  • How often are the flags right? Not known yet. The review protocol is frozen in mesh-review-protocol.md and a blinded sample of 54 segments (30 flagged, 4 debug controls, 20 clean) is drawn and awaiting VC3D review. No precision is claimed until it is labelled.

2 Scan-health stability: measured honestly, then acted on

  • Truly disjoint. The September resample re-read 63% of chunks between "disjoint" runs. With exclusion by construction (64/64 zero overlap), Spearman ρ = 0.63, mean |Δ| = 7.95, top-10 overlap 4/10.
  • Pre-registered v2. A balanced, interleaved sample (48 chunks per run, spread 7) was frozen before any data was read. Verdict FAIL: ρ = 0.750 on 48 eligible volumes, mean |Δ| 2.94, top-10 overlap 7/10, no shared chunk. The systematic run shift fell from +5.46 to +0.25 and Pearson r is 0.911: scores are reliable, but close volumes still swap ranks.
  • Consequence applied. The leaderboard rank column now shows each volume's rank band (best and worst rank across run A, run B and their pooled score). The September scores differ from the v2 pooled score by 6.92 points on average, so the Grand Prize and First Letters frontiers need a new dated campaign; the September artifacts stay frozen. Done 2026-10-04: under v2, PHerc0813 leaves both frontiers; PHerc1447 (Grand Prize) and PHerc0800 (First Letters) are the only robust members (prize-frontier-v2).
  • Known policy sensitivity. The dead-slice penalty sums detections, so it depends on the chunk budget (15 points at 6 chunks, the 30-point cap from 12). Made explicit and versioned, not silently changed.

3 Integrity: missing evidence never trains

scrollq-health now fails closed. An unreadable level, an absent root or a crashed audit is DO NOT TRAIN, never a pass. TRAIN additionally requires finite quality of at least 40/100 and sampling provenance showing every requested chunk decoded. The live re-run reproduces the published verdicts (PHerc0814 DO NOT TRAIN, PHerc0813 TRAIN) and adds an absent-root negative control.

4 Winding and fiber evidence on real data

  • Winding IQ. Published PHercParis4 spiral-input annotations: 2 of 13,700 comparable pairs out of radial order around the umbilicus, both under 0.1 voxel. Injecting one mis-numbered real point at a time, the check catches ±3 errors 179/200, ±5 171/200, and ±2 0/191 by design.
  • Fiber IQ. Reads native VC3D vc3d_fiber JSON v1/v3/v4 and CSV, validates tracer and fallback provenance, can gate CI, and binds to an exact CT root for passports. A frozen public PHercParis4 run covers 8 hash-pinned fibers and 53,828 rendered points: 11 gap and 26 sharp-turn candidates, 0 control-line offsets or order inversions. The public directory is not asserted to name one CT volume, so this is not passport evidence.

5 Formats and documentation

  • Usage by format maps OME-Zarr v3 (sharded) and v2, TIFXYZ, OBJ, PointCollections, fiber CSV/JSON and ink arrays to the command that reads each.
  • Grand Prize readiness map marks every 2027 requirement as implemented evidence, partial, or not yet demonstrated, with links, and deliberately gives no readiness score.
  • VC3D-native review handoff. Diagnostic queues can be converted to the same PointCollections v1 JSON that VC3D loads, so a machine finding can become an in-context human review point without coordinate transcription or a ScrolIQ-specific viewer.

How ScrolIQ compares with existing tools

ScrolIQ does not replace the community's tools; it reads their outputs and checks them against the exact data they claim to describe. Where a tool already does a job well, ScrolIQ consumes its report instead of reimplementing it.

Honest scope

The 0–100 ScrolIQ score is scan-health triage only. It is not readability, surface quality, ink quality, or Grand Prize readiness, and its ranking failed our own stability gate twice. Mesh, winding and fiber flags are review candidates: none has been confirmed by a person in VC3D yet, and nothing has been filed upstream. Local mesh checks do not prove a surface follows the papyrus sheet.

2027 Grand Prize reviewer map

For the fastest review path, start with the Grand Prize readiness map: every major 2027 requirement is marked as implemented evidence, partial, or not yet demonstrated, with a direct link to the artifact or protocol that supports the claim. It deliberately does not turn diagnostics into a readiness score.

What the prize rewards, and what that means for us

The progress prize criteria favour work that is released early, actually gets used, is measured on real data, fixes bugs in tools people use, reveals insightful, actionable information (for analytic tools: shown to have helped unwrapping or ink detection, for example by finding failure cases of existing methods on real scroll data), is well documented, and works with standard community formats.

September covered real data, documentation and formats. The gap is evidence that the flags are correct and that they change what someone does. October therefore prioritises measured precision, the next open-problem layers, and getting the review queues into reviewers' hands. Each goal is merged as soon as it is done rather than held for month end.

Goals

O1Measure how often Mesh IQ flags are real defects

real datafailure cases of existing methodspre-registered · sample drawn

Why. A flag list is only actionable if reviewers can trust it. September found failure candidates on real segments; October must say how many of them are true.

Starting point. 77 of 307 segments flagged with three or more defect kinds; one broken PHerc1447 segment confirmed (corpus-mesh-audit).

Deliverable. The frozen sample of 30 flagged, 4 debugging-control and 20 clean segments, reviewed in VC3D and labelled defect / not a defect / unclear without seeing finding kinds. Publish flag precision and the clean-segment defect rate with intervals.

Done when. Per-segment verdicts, calibration and both rates with 95% intervals are committed. Per-finding-kind rates remain descriptive. Correction, 2026-10-02 UTC: this design does not measure whole-corpus false-negative rate or recall; mixed segments are excluded.

Progress. Protocol frozen in mesh-review-protocol.md. Blinded sample of 54 segments (30 flagged, 4 debug controls, 20 clean) drawn in 2026-10-01-mesh-review-sample/. A deterministic key-free reviewer packet is available via bin/mesh_review_packet.py with instructions in mesh-review-handoff.md. Awaiting VC3D review; no precision exists yet.

O2Winding IQ: patch attachment and cross-collection consistency

real dataopen problem: winding annotationsdone · 6 / 16,074 flagged

Why. Within-collection ray order is clean on PHercParis4. The next errors to catch are chains of relative-winding links that disagree when they meet through shared patches. That is what the spiral fitter's own debugging tool does after a fit; ScrolIQ should do it before one.

Starting point. 2 / 13,700 comparable pairs inverted, both sub-voxel; injection-control recall 89.5% (±3) and 85.5% (±5); ±2 undetectable by design (paris4-winding-ray-order).

Deliverable. Attach annotation points to verified patches, build the winding graph, and report inconsistent cycles with VC3D coordinates. Include the same kind of injection control as the ray-order check, so the error rate it can detect is measured, not assumed.

Done when. A PHercParis4 run with input hashes, a cycle-consistency count, and a measured detection rate for injected inconsistencies.

Result. Pre-registered (winding-attachment-protocol.md) and run on all 20,765 candidate patches: 2,111 of 2,232 points attach within 8 voxels, 206 of 254 relative collections are tied to the absolute frame, and 6 of 16,074 constraints disagree by two or more windings (verdict INCONSISTENT, review queue with VC3D coordinates). The injection control detects 194 / 200 +2 errors (localizes 160). Spiral sense is decided by the data; the branch-cut angle is not (2026-10-03-paris4-winding-attachment/).

O3Spiral IQ: score a fit on constraints it never saw

Implementation update · 2026-10-03 UTC. The held-out geometry evaluator now provides a hash-bound point-comparison interface with declared fit-input exclusion and missing-prediction accounting. This is a foundation, not a completed spiral evaluation: native fitter export, spatial-exclusion evidence and a real fit run remain outstanding.

real dataopen problem: spiral fittinginsightful, actionableplannedneeds a GPU fit run

Why. The fitter reports how well it satisfies the evidence it was given. A held-out score asks the harder question: does the fit predict annotations that were withheld?

Starting point. Spiral IQ is planned only; the held-out geometry split certificate (scrollq-geometry-probe) exists.

Deliverable. Withhold a declared set of winding collections from a fit, then measure the fit against them with the upstream satisfaction tolerances. The provenance gate checks that withheld collections never entered the fit.

Done when. One real held-out evaluation, with the withheld set, fit config and checkpoint hashes committed.

O4Fiber IQ: whole public corpus, then CT support

real dataopen problem: fiber connectivitycorpus done · CT support open

Why. Eight fibers showed that the checks work on real files. To be useful, every published fiber needs auditing, and then a check that a fiber follows real papyrus in the CT.

Starting point. 8 SHA-256-pinned PHercParis4 fibers: 11 gap and 26 sharp-turn candidates, 0 control-line offsets or inversions (public-fiber-audit).

Deliverable. A hash-pinned audit of every public PHercParis4 fiber, then a CT-conditioned orientation and support check with a positive control: a fiber deliberately shifted off the sheet must be flagged.

Done when. The full-corpus artifact is committed, and the CT check catches its positive control without flagging the unshifted fibers it was calibrated on.

Progress, 2026-10-04. Part 1 done: all 136 public PHercParis4 fibers listed and audited, with the 8 pinned fibers as a positive control (identical to their 2026-10-01 results). 388 gap and 519 sharp-turn candidates in 107 fibers; 0 control-line offsets or inversions. All 388 gaps are in the 79 fibers that contain fallback-interpolated spans, and the 57 fully native fibers have none. That is a co-occurrence, not a cause (fiber-corpus-census). Part 2, CT support with a shifted-fiber positive control, needs an exact CT binding and moves to November N4.

O5Scan-health score: pass the stability gate, or retire the ranking

insightful, actionablehonest negative resultsdone · FAIL → rank bands

Why. The published ranking failed our own stability gate. A ranking that reorders on resampling should not be read as an order.

Starting point. Truly-disjoint resample: Spearman ρ = 0.63, mean |Δ| = 7.95, top-10 overlap 4 / 10, against a gate of ρ ≥ 0.85 (truly-disjoint).

Deliverable. Pre-register a sampling design (more chunks, stratified by depth and radius) and its decision rule before any new data is read. Run it truly disjoint. If the gate passes, publish it; if not, replace the rank column on the leaderboard with uncertainty bands.

Done when. The decision rule is applied as written and the leaderboard reflects the result either way.

Progress. Protocol frozen in stability-v2-protocol.md. Published data shows the 0.63 is mostly a sampling-design problem: per-volume run differences are 1.7× wider than chance (SD 10.1 vs 5.82), run 2 sits 5.5 points higher, and the default order's first 12 candidates share one x plane (2026-10-01-stability-v2-prereg/). Version 2 uses a balanced, interleaved order. The run starts once this pre-registration is on main.

Result. Pre-registered verdict FAIL: Spearman ρ = 0.750 on 48 eligible volumes. The leaderboard now shows rank bands. The new sample removed the systematic shift (+5.46 → +0.25) and cut the noise to the level chance predicts, and Pearson r is 0.911, so scores are reliable but close volumes still swap ranks. September scores differ from the v2 pooled score by 6.9 points on average, so the Grand Prize and First Letters frontiers need a new dated campaign (2026-10-stability-v2/).

Ground rules carried from September

  • Every number traces to a command and a committed artifact. Published numbers appear in the README, docs/ and the artifact READMEs, and change together or not at all.
  • Every checker gets a positive control and reports unverified, not ok, when it inspected nothing. A clean audit that checked nothing is a vacuous audit.
  • Pre-register, then log every deviation. Decision rules are frozen before real data is read, and failed gates are published, not rounded away.
  • No readability claims. The 0–100 score is scan-health triage. Surface, mesh, winding, spiral, fiber, label and ink state are never inferred from it.
  • Dated data is frozen data. September artifacts are not edited. October evidence goes in new dated directories.
  • Upstream issues and PyPI releases need the maintainer's explicit approval. Branches are prepared, not shipped.

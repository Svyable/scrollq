Road to the 2027 Grand Prize

ScrolIQ is not counting commits toward a prize. It is tracking the evidence required to turn an exact Herculaneum CT volume into a complete, reproducible, VC3D-compatible, held-out-validated virtual unrolling. Every stage stays measured, partial, unknown, or blocked until the evidence exists.

Vendored libvolcomp decoding made 64 dl.ash2txt.org volcomp volumes content-probeable over HTTP, establishing the first corpus-scale scan-health layer.

Two-phase exclusion produced ρ = 0.63 on truly disjoint chunks. Rather than hide the failure, ScrolIQ tightened the experiment and stopped treating close rank positions as precise.

05 · Limiting dependency

Current bottleneck

The one sentence that should change as soon as the project genuinely advances.
Blocks a qualifying result today
No exact 2027 Grand Prize volume has 100% of its recto surface segmented and unrolled into column-scale meshes with full-column legible ink renders.

The highest-leverage next move is therefore not another dashboard metric. It is an exact-volume physical surface campaign that produces held-out surface predictions, validates them against truth without leakage, and then scales the successful geometry into complete recto coverage.

1 · Prove surface recoveryTransfer the frozen physical-sheet experiment to an exact prize-eligible volume and score held-out surface predictions.
2 · Scale to coverageTurn validated local surface recovery into sequential column-scale TIFXYZ meshes plus detached-patch accounting.
3 · Prove readingRender ink on held-out geometry, quantify falsification controls, then demonstrate letter-by-letter legibility across complete columns.

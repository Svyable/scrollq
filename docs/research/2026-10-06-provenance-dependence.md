The existing scanner-frame equivariance proposal remains complementary. These
probes ask what information is decodable from a representation, rather than
whether predictions commute with an orientation transformation.

For a future counterfactual protocol, cross verified ink/blank × source fragment
× foreign acquisition statistics, keeping the checkpoint frozen. Start with
calibrated monotone intensity maps, bounded measured frequency responses and
normalization variants. Controlled noise is stochastic and noninvertible:
report seed, realization and attenuation of the independently measured ink
signal. Even invertible transforms can destroy practical detectability; an
inverse alone is no preservation certificate. Exclude neural style transfer.
Freeze plausible acquisition bounds and independent morphology-preservation
criteria before predictions are examined. Include identity and inverse-roundtrip
controls, blank false-positive changes, failures and abstentions. A flip under
an unrealistic transform cannot demonstrate an operational shortcut.

Manufactured blinded physical domains could strengthen this design after their
release, licensing, byte inventory and truth custody are independently pinned.
Until then they remain potential controls, not measured evidence.
